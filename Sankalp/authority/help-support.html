❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register a new farmer? ▾
Go to "Register Farmer" in the sidebar. Fill in the farmer's personal, address, and farming details. Capture a photo and fingerprint biometric. Submit to complete registration. The farmer will be assigned a unique Farmer ID automatically.
How do I manage slot capacity? ▾
Navigate to "Slot Management". Select the date and center. Each slot card shows current bookings vs capacity. You can close, reopen, or expand slots as needed.
A farmer forgot to come for their slot. What do I do? ▾
Go to "Farmer Bookings", search for the farmer by name or token number. Mark the booking as "No Show" and the slot will be freed for other farmers.
How are farmers notified about their bookings? ▾
Farmers receive an automated SMS in their selected language with their token number, date, time, and center details. Reminders are sent 1 hour before their slot.
How do I export procurement data? ▾
Go to "Reports & Analytics", select the time period, and click "Export PDF" or "Download CSV" from the mandal performance table. Both formats are supported.
Portal is not loading correctly — what should I do? ▾
Please try clearing your browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+Delete) and refreshing. If the issue persists, contact our IT Helpdesk at 1800-XXX-TECH or email support@sankalp.gov.in.
