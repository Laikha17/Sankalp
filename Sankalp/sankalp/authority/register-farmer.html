❓ How It Works?

1

Select Language

Choose your preferred language (Telugu / Hindi / English).
🔊 "Please select your language... 1. తెలుగు 2. हिंदी 3. English"
2

Enter Details

Provide your Mandal and choose the date (at least 3 days in advance).
📅 "Please select a date for slot booking. Slots are available only from 3 days onwards."
3

Choose Slot

Listen to available slot timings and select your preferred slot.
🕐 "Available slots for 15 September are... 1. 09:00–10:00 AM 2. 10:00–11:00 AM 3. 11:00–12:00 PM ..."
4

Confirm Booking

Confirm your selected date and slot.
✅ "You have selected 15 September, 10:00 AM–11:00 AM. Press 1 to confirm."
5

Get Confirmation

You will receive a confirmation SMS with your token number, date, time and center details.
📱 "Your slot is successfully booked! Token No: 27 | Date: 15 Sep 2026 | Time: 10:00 AM | Center: Nalgonda Procurement Center"
ℹ️ Important: Farmers can book slots only up to 3 days in advance.

📞 Offline Phone Call Registration

For farmers who are not available online, they will call 3 days before crop delivery. The automated system will guide them.
