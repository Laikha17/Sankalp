❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a procurement slot? ▾
You can book a slot online through this portal by clicking "Book Slot" in the menu. Alternatively, call our toll-free number 1800-XXX-XXXX and follow the automated steps.
Can I book a slot via phone call? ▾
Yes! Call 1800-XXX-XXXX, select your language, and the automated system will guide you to select date, time, and confirm your slot. You'll receive an SMS confirmation.
How many days in advance can I book? ▾
You can book a slot up to 7 days in advance. Slots open 3 days before the date, so ensure you book well ahead.
What documents do I need at the center? ▾
Please bring your original Aadhaar card, land ownership documents (Pattadar Passbook), and produce. Your biometric verification will be done on-site.
When will I receive payment? ▾
Payment is typically processed within 3-5 working days after quality verification at the center. You'll receive an SMS notification when it's done.
Can I cancel or reschedule my slot? ▾
Yes, you can cancel or reschedule up to 2 hours before your booked slot. Use the "Book Slot" page or call the toll-free number for assistance.
